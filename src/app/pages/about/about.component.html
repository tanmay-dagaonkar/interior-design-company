About Karigrah

Design rooted in craftsmanship.

Karigrah began with a simple belief: a home should be made, not just decorated. We're a small studio of designers and makers working across residential and hospitality interiors.

Our Story

From a small workshop to full-scale interiors.

Karigrah started as a furniture workshop, working with a handful of skilled artisans on custom pieces. Clients kept asking for more — for the whole room, then the whole home — and the studio grew from there.

Today we design and execute complete interiors, but the workshop mindset never left. Every project still starts with a question: what can be made well, by hand, for this specific space?
