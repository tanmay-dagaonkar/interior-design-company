Our Story

Design and execution, under one roof.

Karigrah is Jabalpur's premium interior design studio, working across residential and commercial interiors — from full-home makeovers and modular kitchens to office interiors, home bars, and renovations. The approach stays the same regardless of project size: understand the real requirement first, then design and build around it.

Instead of handing clients off between a designer, a contractor, and a site supervisor, Karigrah runs design, materials, and on-site execution as one team — so the only thing you have to manage is the decision-making.

Budgets are planned and explained upfront, not discovered halfway through a project. Design isn't just what you see — it's the material quality, craftsmanship, and detailing you don't see, which is exactly where most cost surprises hide.
