Our Philosophy

Design that respects craftsmanship.

The word "Karigrah" draws from "kaarigar" — the artisan. Every project we take on is built around that idea: thoughtful design paired with honest, skilled craft. We work closely with local makers, sourcing materials responsibly, to create interiors that feel personal rather than trend-driven.
